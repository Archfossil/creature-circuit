Stat Suite: Vigor, Health, Vitality, Power, Armor, Focus, Ward, Speed, Stamina, and passive Abilities / Natures (e.g., Adamant: +Power, -Focus).

Type Chart Matrix: 8-type system with defined 2x / 1x / 0.5x damage multipliers and match-up interactions.


# Attributes Detailed

Each creature has the following attributes with sub-attributes in parenthesis.

* Vigor (Health and Vitality)
* Power & Armor
* Focus & Ward
* Speed
* Stamina (Fatigue)

## Vigor

The creature's Vigor informs the Health and Vitality of the creature. (A creature with 3 Vigor has 3 Health and 3 Vitality by default.) Incoming damage is dealt to Health first. When Health hits zero, a point of Vitality is removed and Health is reset.
