Action Economy: Players get 5 actions per turn to spend on movement, capture attempts, training, or resting.

Energy System: Creatures generate elemental energy per turn based on their stats (e.g., Charm [C], Rock [R], Neutral [X]). Moves cost specific energy combinations (e.g., 3 Fire + 1 Any).

Custom Moves & Cards: Designed with frontside metadata (art, base stats, energy generation, passive Ability, Nature) and backside flowchart progression nodes. (Note: Data entries for creatures and moves will be provided in a later phase).
