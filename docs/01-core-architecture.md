# Action Economy
-- Players get 5 Energy per turn to spend on movement, exploring map nodes, or performing city actions.
-- Some actions cost more Energy. Some trainer abilities may influence energy costs. Trainers who prefer nature may not spend an energy to re-explore a space, but city actions cost an additional Energy, while trainers who prefer urban environments may have this interaction inverted.

# Map Traversal
-- 1 Energy to move to an adjacent map node.
-- There are 8 cities in the map, each inspired by a different American national park and representing a different type in our type chart which the City Arbiter uses as their main creature type for challengers. In a City players can rest at a stable. Each stable provides a Chanko Nabe (Hot Pot) to recover Creatures and provide unique buffs based on the city / stable. Players can set a City as their "home" where they will return to in case of a total team wipe. Changing your home affiliation can affect the morale of your Creatures.




Action Economy: Players get 5 actions per turn to spend on movement, capture attempts, training, or resting.

Energy System: Creatures generate elemental energy per turn based on their stats (e.g., Charm [C], Rock [R], Neutral [X]). Moves cost specific energy combinations (e.g., 3 Fire + 1 Any).

Custom Moves & Cards: Designed with frontside metadata (art, base stats, energy generation, passive Ability, Nature) and backside flowchart progression nodes. (Note: Data entries for creatures and moves will be provided in a later phase).
